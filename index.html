Our Journey

Five Summers Along the Battery

From an unexpected rainstorm in the historic French Quarter to forever under the live oaks.

We believe the best chapters in life begin without a script.

Our story started on an unseasonably warm afternoon five summers ago outside a small antiquarian bookstore on Church Street. When a sudden Charleston thunderstorm rolled across the harbor, we both ducked beneath the same striped awning, striking up a conversation about historic architecture, Lowcountry waterways, and our mutual obsession with local coffee.

Those first few hours unfolded into years of shared sunrise walks along the Battery, restoring our 1920s craftsman home, navigating tidal marshes in a wooden skiff, and discovering that life is infinitely richer together.

In October 2025, during an evening stroll by White Point Garden, Marcus asked Sophia to share a lifetime of adventures together. We could not imagine a more meaningful place to exchange our vows than right here in Charleston, surrounded by the family and friends who have shaped our lives.

Ceremony & Reception Setting

Both our ceremony and evening celebration will take place on the grounds of The Marlowe House. The ceremony is outdoors on the lawn, followed by covered veranda cocktails and courtyard dining.

We invite guests to dress in formal evening attire. Lightweight tuxedos or dark suits for gentlemen, and elegant floor-length or formal midi gowns for ladies. Breathable formal fabrics are encouraged for the June warmth.
